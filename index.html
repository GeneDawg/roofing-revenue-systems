    Workable Order (What Each Offering Does)

    This is the operational order for Peak 301 contractors. The platform is designed so you can start small, then add enterprise services only when needed.

    1) PayPixl Lead Origination (Interactive Proof + Lead Sheet)

    Capture roofs and generate an AI-assessed, homeowner-facing “proof” experience with clickable damage markers + explainer content, and a contractor-facing lead sheet with the property/owner data packet used to market the lead.

    2) City Publications Direct Mail (QR → Proof → Quote)

    Print and mail targeted pieces to the exact properties sourced from PayPixl capture + data. Each mailer uses a QR code that routes the homeowner into the interactive roof proof and your quote workflow (repair / rejuvenate / replacement).

    3) Multi-Channel Outreach Engine (Follow-Up)

    After mail hits: text + email + cold email + call center + door knock can be layered to drive immediate appointments. Peak Pros can also build cold email campaigns for contractors as a paid service.

    4) Triage: Repair • Rejuvenate • Replace (White-Label Offshoot)

    Every lead gets classified: Repair (minor), Rejuvenate (Peak 301 fit), or Replace (storm/age/condition). White-label rejuvenation activates here as a stand-alone high-margin offering.

    5) Claims Strategy (When Replace = Claim-Eligible)

    If there is damage sufficient for a claim, the platform routes the file into claims strategy and documentation posture, improving scope clarity, reducing leakage, and accelerating settlement momentum.

    6) Settlement Services + Disbursement Control (IINK)

    When funds move, controlled disbursement and milestone logic reduces disputes, increases production velocity, and keeps cash predictable. This also creates cleaner audit trails for all parties.

    7) Legal Escalation (WEK) — Only If Carrier/Process Breaks

    If a file stalls or becomes a true escalation scenario, the platform can route the homeowner into an appropriate legal pathway. This is the “last mile” tool — used selectively.

    8) Company Health + Valuation Lift (TradeGauge.ai)

    Pricing, margin discipline, and operational consistency raise contractor health — which supports long-term value creation and a pathway toward stronger sale-readiness (if/when a contractor wants private equity interest).

    9) Back Office + Call Center (Add-Ons)

    Add digital back office support and call center capacity to increase speed to contact, improve booking rate, and reduce owner/operator load. These are optional add-ons once lead flow is proven.
