    Plug-and-Play Workflow (Contractor-Ready)

    This is the order Peak 301 contractors actually execute. RPM enables the platform; PayPixl + CityPub produce demand; triage monetizes every roof.

    1) RPM Territory + Onboarding

    Contractor becomes equipped and trained to deliver Peak 301 correctly.

    • Territory procurement + certification pathway
    • Equipment + Peak 301 solution procurement
    • Training portal + standards that protect outcomes

    2) PayPixl Origination

    AI roof assessment + clickable proof + contractor lead sheet.

    • Homeowner-facing proof (AI markers + explainers)
    • Contractor lead sheet (parcel/owner/mailing address)
    • Storm-event history supports timing + triage

    3) City Publications QR Mailers

    Mail to verified addresses. QR sends homeowner to “their roof” proof and your intake.

    • 6×11 campaigns tied directly to PayPixl lists
    • QR conversion rail: proof → selection → contact
    • Batch scaling (1k / 5k / 10k drops)

    4) Classification: Repair / Rejuvenate / Replace

    No wasted lead. Every roof becomes a lane.

    • Repair: fast cash + high close rate
    • Rejuvenate: Peak 301 margin lane
    • Replace: retail or insurance path

    5) Peak Pros White Label Program (Add-On)

    Contractor can deploy rejuvenation under their brand or Peak Pros.

    • Enterprise pricing + playbooks
    • Revenue share options (where applicable)
    • Upsell that increases territory value

    6) Claims + Settlement + Legal + Profit Health

    When damage warrants: claims support, money control, legal escalation, and PE readiness.

    • Claims support: 5th Element (where permitted)
    • Disbursement: iink (settlement workflow)
    • Legal escalation: WEK (when appropriate)
    • Profit + exit pathway: TradeGauge.ai
